Citizen North is a full-service hospitality production company. For hotel groups, openings and repositionings, we run the whole production in-house: concept, shot lists, styling, casting, hair and makeup, crew, photography, video and post-production. One accountable team delivers an asset library built around your marketing plan for years to come.

Everything in-house

  • Creative direction and concept
  • Shot lists, call sheets and scheduling
  • Location scouting on property
  • Styling, wardrobe and props
  • Casting and talent management
  • Hair and makeup
  • Architectural and lifestyle photography
  • Video and drone
  • Editing, retouching and colour grading
  • Delivery, licensing and asset organization

Why one team matters

Hiring separate photographers, videographers, stylists, casting agents and hair and makeup artists means more coordination, more cost and more risk of the pieces not matching. With Citizen North, every role works from the same plan, on the same schedule, toward the same brand.

Pre-production

Every production starts with a creative brief, shot list, styling and props guide, casting plan and call sheet, all approved by your team before we arrive.

Multi-property campaigns

For hotel groups, we plan consistent imagery across several properties in one campaign, so your brand looks the same in every location.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between a photographer and a production company?

A photographer takes the pictures. A production company like Citizen North also handles creative direction, styling, casting, hair and makeup, video, scheduling and post-production, so the whole shoot is planned and delivered by one team.

What does Citizen North handle in-house?

Creative direction, shot lists, styling and props, casting, hair and makeup, photography, video, drone, editing and colour grading.

Can you produce a campaign across several hotels?

Yes. We plan multi-property campaigns with one creative direction so every property matches.

Do you work with our agency or in-house brand team?

Yes. We can lead the creative or produce to an agency's brief.
