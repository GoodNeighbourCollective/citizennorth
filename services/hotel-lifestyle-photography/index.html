Lifestyle photography

Hotel lifestyle photography

Lifestyle photography shows guests what it feels like to stay: the morning swim, dinner on the terrace, the hour in the spa. Citizen North casts, styles and directs every scene in-house, so the imagery sells the experience, not just the room.

Casting and talent, in-house

We cast talent that fits your brand and your guest, from couples and families to wellness and adventure travellers. Casting, talent booking and model releases are all handled by our team.

Styling, hair and makeup

Our stylists plan wardrobe, props and set dressing in a styling guide approved before the shoot. Our hair and makeup artists are on set for every lifestyle production, so talent looks right in every frame.

Dining, spa and experiences

We photograph the moments that drive bookings: dining, spa treatments, pool days, activities and the details guests remember.

One production with your architectural shoot

Lifestyle and architectural photography are planned and shot together by one crew, so the library is consistent and you pay for one production.

Every production, fully in-house. Creative direction, shot lists, styling and props, casting, hair and makeup, photography, video, drone and post-production. One team, one plan, one invoice.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does Citizen North provide models?

Yes. Casting is handled in-house. We source and book talent to match your brand and guest profile, and manage all model releases.

Do you provide hair and makeup?

Yes. Hair and makeup artists are part of our in-house production team and are on set for every lifestyle shoot.

Can we use staff or real guests instead of models?

Yes. We can direct staff or guests who agree to take part, and we handle releases either way.

Should we shoot lifestyle and architecture together?

Usually, yes. One production costs less than two, and the imagery matches in light, colour and styling.
