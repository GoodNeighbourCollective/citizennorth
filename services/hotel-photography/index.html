Luxury hotel and resort photography

Citizen North photographs luxury hotels and resorts for brand websites, booking platforms, sales and press. We shoot interiors, exteriors, suites, public spaces and aerials, timed to each property's best light, and deliver images that meet Fairmont and Hilton brand standards.

What we photograph

Guest rooms and suites, lobbies and public spaces, pools and beaches, spas, restaurants and bars, meeting and event spaces, exteriors at dusk, and aerial views of the full property.

Planned around your guests and your light

We build the shot list with your team, then schedule each space for its best light and lowest occupancy. Rooms are styled by our in-house stylists before every frame, so the property looks its best without your staff stopping their day.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many images does a hotel photo shoot deliver?

It depends on the property's size and the number of shoot days. A full-property production usually delivers a library of more than one hundred final images; our Fairmont Mayakoba production delivered more than 200.

Can you photograph a hotel while it's open?

Yes. We schedule each space around occupancy and work quietly around guests. Most of our hotel shoots happen while the property is fully operating.

Do you shoot to Fairmont, Hilton and Marriott brand standards?

Yes. We are an approved vendor for Fairmont and Hilton and build every shot list from the brand's current photography guidelines.

Do you style the rooms?

Yes. Styling and props are handled in-house by our own stylists, planned in a styling guide before the shoot.
