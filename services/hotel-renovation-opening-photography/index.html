Openings and renovations

New hotel opening and renovation photography

A renovation, rebrand or opening makes every existing image out of date at once. Citizen North plans photography and video around your timeline, from architectural images of finished spaces to full lifestyle campaigns once the property is operating, so new imagery is live when bookings open.

Phased shoots

Pre-opening: architecture and interiors as spaces are completed. Opening: lifestyle, dining and spa once the hotel is operating. Launch: a full campaign with video for marketing and PR.

Renovated rooms, while you stay open

We photograph renovated floors and suites as they come online, without disrupting guests in the rest of the hotel.

Rebrands and flag changes

When a property changes brand, we produce imagery to the new brand's standards so you're ready for its booking channels on day one.

Working with owners and management companies

We work with owners, developers, brand teams and management companies, and fit into existing procurement and vendor processes.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When should we book photography for a hotel opening?

Three to six months before opening. That allows pre-production, and lets us phase the shoot as spaces are completed.

Can you shoot before construction is fully finished?

Yes. We schedule finished spaces first and return for the rest, or plan angles that avoid unfinished areas.

Can you photograph renovated rooms while the hotel is open?

Yes. We work floor by floor around occupancy.

Do you work with hotel management companies?

Yes. We work with owners, brands and third-party management companies and can complete vendor onboarding before the project starts.
