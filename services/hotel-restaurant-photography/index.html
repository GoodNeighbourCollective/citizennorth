Food and beverage

Hotel restaurant and bar photography

Hotel restaurants and bars need imagery that works for menus, reservation platforms, press and social. Citizen North photographs dishes, cocktails, chefs and dining rooms in their real setting, styled in-house to the restaurant's concept and the hotel's brand.

Dishes and cocktails

We photograph signature dishes and cocktails close up, lit to show texture and colour. Each plate is styled with the kitchen team, so it looks the way it's served.

Dining rooms and bars

We shoot each room at the hour it feels best, set and lit for service. The images show guests the atmosphere before they book a table.

Chefs and teams

Portraits and working shots of your chefs, bartenders and floor team. They give press and social a face behind the food.

Dining lifestyle scenes

We cast and style guests at the table, so the imagery shows the experience, not just the plate. Casting, styling, hair and makeup are all handled in-house.

Every production, fully in-house. Creative direction, shot lists, styling and props, casting, hair and makeup, photography, video, drone and post-production. One team, one plan, one invoice.

Frequently asked questions

Do you style the food?

Yes. Food and drinks are styled with your kitchen team and our in-house stylists, so every plate looks the way it is served.

Can you shoot during service?

We shoot before or between services, so guests and the kitchen are not disrupted.

Can the restaurant be part of a full hotel shoot?

Yes. Adding it to a full hotel production usually costs less than a separate restaurant shoot.
