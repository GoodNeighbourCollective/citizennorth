FAQ

Questions hotel marketing teams ask us

Is Citizen North an approved vendor for Fairmont and Hilton?

Yes. Citizen North is an approved photography and video vendor for Fairmont Hotels & Resorts and for Hilton. We are also registered in the Marriott, IHG, Hyatt and Wyndham vendor programs.

What does Citizen North handle in-house?

Everything. Citizen North handles creative direction, shot lists, styling and props, casting, hair and makeup, architectural photography, lifestyle photography, video, drone, editing and colour grading in-house. You deal with one team and one invoice.

Do you shoot photography and video in the same production?

Yes. We plan stills and motion together and shoot them with one crew, so your photos and film match and you pay for one production instead of two.

Where do you work?

Citizen North has crew in Vancouver, Los Angeles, Toronto, Montreal, London and Hong Kong, and produces shoots across the United States, Canada, Mexico, the Caribbean, Europe and Asia.

How far ahead should we book a hotel shoot?

For a full campaign, two to four months ahead. That leaves time for creative direction, styling, casting, and scheduling around occupancy and the best light.

When should a hotel update its photography?

After a renovation, a rebrand or a new opening, or when your imagery is more than three to four years old and no longer matches the guest experience.
