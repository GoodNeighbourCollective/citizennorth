Where jungle
meets the
Caribbean.

Set within a private estate on Mexico's Riviera Maya, Fairmont Mayakoba is a sanctuary where tropical jungle, freshwater canals, and white-sand beach converge. Citizen North was commissioned to produce architectural and aerial photography that captures the scale, serenity, and distinctive sense of place that defines this world-class resort.

The Challenge

Scale without
losing intimacy.

Fairmont Mayakoba spans over 240 acres of protected coastal land. The challenge was to convey the grandeur of the property — its sprawling pools, oceanfront suites, and lush mangrove waterways — while preserving the intimate, secluded atmosphere that makes the guest experience so distinctive.

Aerial photography was essential to revealing the relationship between the resort's architecture and its natural surroundings — something impossible to appreciate from ground level alone.

Our Approach

Golden hour
from every angle.

We planned every shoot around the light. Ground-level architectural photography was captured during the soft, directional light of early morning and late afternoon — moments when the resort's clean lines and natural materials come alive with warmth and texture.

Aerial sessions were timed to coincide with the lowest sun angles, producing long shadows and rich contrast that reveal the geometry of the pools, pathways, and rooftops against the deep turquoise of the Caribbean.

The Work

Architecture
in its element.

The final collection includes over 200 architectural and aerial photography assets — exteriors, interiors, pool and beach areas, spa facilities, and dining spaces — all captured to highlight the resort's seamless integration with its tropical environment.

Deliverables were produced in multiple formats for use across Fairmont's global marketing channels, travel publications, and digital platforms.

"The aerial perspectives brought an entirely new dimension to how we present our property. Truly exceptional work."
— Director of Marketing, Fairmont Mayakoba
