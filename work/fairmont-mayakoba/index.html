The brief

Fairmont Mayakoba needed a new image library covering the resort's architecture, pools, beach, spa, dining and guest experience, for its website, booking channels, sales materials and press. The property spans more than 240 acres of jungle, canals and beachfront. The challenge was to show that scale while keeping the private, secluded feeling guests come for.

02

Our approach

We planned the production around light and occupancy. Before arriving, we built a shot list with the marketing team and a styling and props guide for every lifestyle scene. Architecture was shot in early morning and late afternoon light. Aerials were timed for low sun angles that reveal the geometry of the pools and rooftops against the Caribbean. Lifestyle scenes were cast, styled and directed in-house to Fairmont's brand standards.

03

What we delivered

A library of more than 200 images: exteriors, suites, pools and beach, spa, dining, lifestyle and aerials, in formats for web, booking platforms, print and social.
